------ TASK B ------
Proving f(r -> 0) = r-r^2 -> 0
Assume f(r) can be approximated by a power expansion such that:
f(r) = a_0 + a_1 r + a_2 r^2 + ...
f''(r) = 2 a_2 + 6 a_3 r + 12 a_4 r^2 + ...
The s-wave radial Schrodinger equation is:
-(1/2) f'' -(1/r) f = e f
or equivalently:
f'' + (2/r)f + 2ef = 0
Inserting the power series gives a singular term proportional to a_0/r,
so regularity at r=0 requires a_0 = 0.
The constant term then gives 2a_2 + 2a_1 = 0, hence a_2 = -a_1.
Therefore f(r) = a_1(r-r^2+...) and so f(r->0)->0.
